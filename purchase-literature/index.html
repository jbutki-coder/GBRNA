Literature Outreach to Africa

NA Book Drive prints and distributes draft and alternative NA literature at a higher cost than some other sources. Its purpose is to use the proceeds to send mini Fifth Edition Basic Texts (Book One) to members in Africa.

Reported Basic Text prices include 1,646 Kenyan shillings in Kenya and R210 (South African rand) in South Africa. Costs like these can put literature out of reach for members. Local prices may change.

Contact NA Book Drive directly to ask about ordering literature or supporting its book distribution.

These independent literature sources are listed for members' information. GBRNA does not sell these books, collect payments, or fulfill orders. A listing does not imply affiliation or endorsement. Confirm all ordering details directly with the group.
