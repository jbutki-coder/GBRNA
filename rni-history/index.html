How the mapping works

The NY project edition remains the baseline calendar used on GBRNA. Earlier material without final calendar dates is attached to a day only when its Grey Book quotation/source can be matched confidently. Material that cannot be placed confidently is preserved below as Unmapped Historical R&I rather than guessed.

The Grateful Wake Up Group document specifically says its printed dates are for historical purposes, so those dates are retained as writing-history information and are not used as the final GBR calendar placement.
