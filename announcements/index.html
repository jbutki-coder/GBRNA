Okanagan NA: Monday & Thursday Evening Time Update

The Monday and Thursday night Okanagan NA meetings will follow British Columbia time: 7:30 PM year-round (UTC−7), with no daylight saving clock changes.

Current meeting times: 9:30 PM Eastern (EST) · 8:30 PM Central (CST) · 7:30 PM Mountain (MST) · 6:30 PM Pacific (PST), on Monday and Thursday.
