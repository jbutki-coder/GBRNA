Unifying “We” With Traditions

April 9–11, 2027 | Branson, Missouri

Recovery • Unity • Service

Download the event flyer

NA members and groups will gather in Branson, Missouri, for a weekend dedicated to fellowship unity, the Fifth Tradition, the Twelfth Step, and the practical application of our spiritual principles.

One of the gathering's central activities is an effort to prepare and distribute 30 boxes of NA Basic Texts to 30 jails across Arizona, Oklahoma, and Missouri.

The event reflects a growing interest among NA members and groups in taking a more direct role in making recovery literature accessible, including efforts to print and distribute literature through the collective resources of participating groups.

Printing Literature and the Seventh Tradition

The Seventh Tradition reminds us that every NA group ought to be fully self-supporting, declining outside contributions.

Self-support involves more than placing money in a basket. It also includes the time, effort, skills, and resources members voluntarily contribute toward fulfilling our primary purpose.

When groups choose to devote their resources to producing and providing literature for addicts who might otherwise go without it, they demonstrate how self-support and carrying the message can work together.

The message of recovery should be accessible to the addict who needs it, regardless of that addict's financial circumstances.

Through group conscience, cooperation, and voluntary service, groups can explore practical ways to make literature more readily available, while remaining mindful of the responsibilities involved in its reproduction and distribution.

The gathering also welcomes groups regardless of which edition of the Basic Text they use, emphasizing shared spiritual principles over disagreements.

Same Program. Different Groups. One Primary Purpose.
