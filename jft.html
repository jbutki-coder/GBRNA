About Just For Tonight

Evening Meditation, Literature Study, and Group Review & Input

Just For Tonight is an evening meditation literature project of The Core Group of Narcotics Anonymous. It is presented here for daily reading, recovery, literature study, discussion, correction, and input from NA members and Groups.

What this page is

A daily reading and study page for the current Just For Tonight draft, including audio where available, archives, search, downloads, review forms, and related historical literature resources.

The Grey Area Group hosts this page as a service. Hosting does not change the project's origin, current literature standing, or the autonomy of any participating NA Group.

The purpose of the project

To offer an evening meditation that helps members close the day in recovery and prepare to continue living the program tomorrow.

Each reading may be reviewed on its own page. Corrections, source information, Group input, and suggested changes can be submitted through the Review & Input form displayed with the reading.

Literature standing

Draft and Review

The project remains open to review, correction, discussion, and Group input and should not be represented as finally approved fellowship literature.

Principles behind this work

Open any heading for more information about how this project is presented and reviewed.

Daily Recovery

The readings are organized by calendar date so members can use the project as a consistent evening recovery practice while also browsing or searching the complete draft.

Review & Input

Every reading includes a form for corrections, source details, editorial suggestions, and input from individual members or NA Groups.

Home-Group Autonomy

Each NA Home Group remains free to review this material, use or decline it, suggest changes, and reach its own Group conscience.

Direct Responsibility

Service remains accountable to the members and Groups being served. Project identity and literature standing are stated plainly so readers can make informed decisions.

Clear Literature Standing

Draft, Review & Input, historically approved, home-group-approved, and broadly approved literature should be identified separately rather than presented as though each has the same standing.

Service Without Control

Hosting, formatting, and distributing the material are service functions. They do not transfer authority over the literature away from its project or the NA Groups reviewing it.

Autonomy & Service Documents

These drafts present a home-group-autonomy approach to NA service and literature. They are provided for review, discussion, editing, and Group conscience, not as adopted policy unless a Group formally approves them.
