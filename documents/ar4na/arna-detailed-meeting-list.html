Historical directory disclaimer: This list reflects May 27, 2024, not the current AR4NA meeting schedule. Some groups no longer meet, and times, locations, access links, and service meetings may be out of date. Confirm details directly with each group. Okanagan's Zoom credentials alone were updated on October 24, 2026; its archived schedule below has not been reverified. See Okanagan's current schedule. See GBRNA's current meeting directory.

Time-zone rule: A meeting without a listed time zone is marked Pacific (presumed), as requested. Explicit zones are preserved exactly as posted: PST, EST, MST, GMT, and IST. These archived abbreviations may not reliably describe daylight-saving practice. Times have not been silently converted or corrected.

This is a historical directory, not verification that a meeting is still active. Duplicate occurrences have been consolidated. Conflicting times, missing days, unclear online access, and unreadable ending times are flagged.

Physical addresses behind map links were recovered from those links. A virtual label paired only with a map link is marked unclear rather than supplied with invented Zoom details.

AR4NA History & Business Meeting Minutes

Following the closure of the former ARNA organization based in San Jose, California, the service effort moved forward as the Autonomous Region FOR Narcotics Anonymous (AR4NA) in Pennsylvania. The September 1, 2026 business minutes explain that AR4NA was incorporated in Pennsylvania on November 1, 2022, and the California ARNA corporation was terminated on April 2, 2026. The minutes distinguish the two as separate organizations. The September 29 minutes report a new AR4NA mailing address in Philadelphia.

Source and interpretation notes

  • Original archived ARNA meetings page — May 27, 2024.
  • All 85 source recovery-meeting rows are represented, with repeated daily entries and duplicates consolidated. Service meetings are listed separately.
  • Written by Addicts for Addicts has two incompatible time-zone listings for the same Zoom ID; both are retained.
